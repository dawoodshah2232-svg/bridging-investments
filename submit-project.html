What we look for

Evaluation criteria

  • Real cash flow. Contracted or evidenced revenue — not projections alone.
  • Clean title. Verifiable ownership, no undisclosed encumbrances.
  • Skin in the game. Sponsor co-investment or meaningful retained risk.
  • Operable. A credible operator with a track record we can check.
  • Reportable. Books we can reconcile monthly and publish.
