The investor journey

From interest to income,
in eight steps.

No jargon, no blurred lines. You will always know what is a hold, what is a commitment, and what creates ownership.

1 · Explore

Browse approved projects. Economics, fees, risks, operator and full documents are public before you commit to anything.

2 · Verify

Confirm identity, residence and eligibility. Our compliance team reviews every profile — no reservation without it.

3 · Reserve

Choose units, review the current document version, confirm. Units are held for you with a visible countdown — a hold, not ownership.

4 · Fund

When a project is fully reserved, a funding call issues your unique payment reference. Bank transfer only; every dirham is reconciled.

5 · Close

Funding, documents and conditions verified — legal closing and issuance into the project company's register. Now you are an owner.

6 · Operate

The project runs. You receive monthly reports, project updates and approved distribution notices in your portal.

7 · Earn

Distributions are paid only from real, legally distributable profit and available cash — to your verified account.

8 · Exit

Transfers to eligible buyers, asset sale or wind-up — all under documented rules, with final accounts published.

Good to know

The fine print, up front.

📌

Binding vs non-binding

Exploring and registering interest are non-binding. Confirming a reservation is a commitment to fund within the disclosed window. Ownership arises only from cleared funds and legal issuance.

🎯

All-or-nothing closing

Campaigns close only at 100% funding. If the long-stop date passes short of target, the offer is cancelled and collected subscriptions are returned under the refund policy.

💳

Fees, disclosed

1% of capital at closing plus 1% annual administration — shown with amounts, recipients and timing on every project page. No hidden spreads, no surprises.
