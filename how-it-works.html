The investor journey

From interest to income,
in eight steps.

No jargon, no blurred lines. You will always know what is a hold, what is a commitment, and what creates ownership.

01Explore

View permitted project information, risks, capital budget, fees and status. Register interest if the offer is unavailable to you.

02Verify

Verify contact details, identity, residence, tax information, funding source and eligibility. Reviews are resolved before you can reserve.

03Reserve

Select quantity, review the current documents and confirm terms. Units are held for you with a visible expiry time, and you receive a reservation reference.

04Reach target

When valid commitments cover the offer, it is labelled fully reserved awaiting payment. Finance and compliance approve the funding call.

05Pay

A unique payment reference and due date are issued. You pay the authorised designated account; uploaded proof remains unverified until reconciliation.

06Close

Funding, eligibility, signed documents, remaining statutory rights and acquisition conditions are confirmed. Then legal closing and ownership issuance.

07Operate

Your portal shows issued holdings, the actual start date, monthly reports, approved distribution notices and project updates.

08Exit

A permitted transfer, asset sale or winding up — administered under the legal waterfall. The final account is published and net proceeds distributed.

The three rules

What shapes every step above

  1. A reservation is a hold, not ownership. It reduces available slots; cleared funds and completed legal issuance create ownership.
  2. Full reservation triggers a payment call. Acquisition begins only after funding and all closing conditions are satisfied.
  3. Reports monthly; distributions from real profit only. Paid only from legally distributable profits and available cash after approved costs and reserves. Capital and returns are at risk.
Good to know

The fine print, up front.

📌

Binding vs non-binding

Exploring and registering interest are non-binding. Confirming a reservation is a commitment to fund within the disclosed window. Ownership arises only from cleared funds and legal issuance.

🎯

All-or-nothing closing

Campaigns close only at 100% funding. If the long-stop date passes short of target, the offer is cancelled and collected subscriptions are returned under the refund policy.

💳

Fees, disclosed

1% of capital at closing plus 1% annual administration — shown with amounts, recipients and timing on every project page. No hidden spreads, no surprises.
