FAQs

Straight answers.

The questions every investor should ask — answered without spin.

No. A reservation reduces available units and holds your place with a visible expiry. Ownership is created only when funds clear, documents are signed and the legal issuance is recorded in the project company's register.
Distributions are paid only from legally distributable profit and available cash, after costs and reserves, on an approved monthly cycle. They are never automatic and never guaranteed — a bad month means no distribution.
Holdings are illiquid — there is no public market or sell button. Transfers to an eligible, verified buyer are possible under documented rules, subject to lock-ups and approvals. Plan for the full holding period.
1% of capital at legal closing plus 1% annual administration, charged to the project company. Every project page shows the amounts, recipients and timing before you reserve. No hidden spreads.
We use all-or-nothing closing. If the long-stop date passes without 100% funding, the offer is cancelled and collected subscriptions are returned under the disclosed refund policy — typically within 10 business days.
Project money sits in the project company's own accounts, moved only through our authorised money-flow partner. Platform operating money is fully separate — never mixed.
