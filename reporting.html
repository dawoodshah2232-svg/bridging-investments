Reporting & transparency

Every dirham,
accounted monthly.

Reports publish on the 15th of every month. Figures are never overwritten — corrections are published as new versions with the change log visible.

The monthly close

A timetable we keep.

Day 1–5

Operator submits revenue, costs and bank statements. Finance reconciles every line to source documents.

Day 6–10

Draft report prepared — profit, cash movement, reserves and proposed distribution, with the full waterfall.

Day 11–13

Compliance and a second approver review. Nothing publishes on one signature.

Day 15

Report published to your portal. Distribution notices follow the approved cycle.

Cash available for distribution

How to read this

The same table appears in every monthly report

Start with revenue. Subtract operating costs (including disclosed management fees) to reach net profit. Then adjust for non-cash items, debt repayments, capital spending and reserve movements. What remains is cash available for distribution — the only number a payout can come from.
