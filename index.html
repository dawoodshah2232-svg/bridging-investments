How ownership works

From interest to income,
in eight clear steps.

A reservation holds units. Cleared funds plus legal issuance create ownership. Nothing in between is blurred.

Explore

Browse approved projects — economics, risks, fees and documents, all public.

Verify

Confirm identity, residence and eligibility. Compliance reviews before you can reserve.

Reserve

Pick units, review documents, confirm. Units are held with a visible expiry timer.

Fund

When fully reserved, a funding call issues your unique payment reference.

Close

Funding, documents and conditions confirmed — legal closing and issuance.

Operate

Monthly reports, project updates and approved distribution notices.

Earn

Distributions paid only from real, distributable profit to verified accounts.

Exit

Transfers, asset sale or wind-up under documented rules. Final accounts published.

No. A reservation reduces available units and holds your place while you pay. Ownership is created only when funds clear, documents are signed and the legal issuance is recorded in the project company's register.
Distributions are paid only from legally distributable profit and available cash, after costs and reserves. Reports are monthly; payouts follow an approved monthly cycle — never automatic, never guaranteed.
Holdings are illiquid — there is no public market. Transfers are possible to an eligible buyer under documented rules, subject to lock-ups and approvals. Plan for the full holding period.
