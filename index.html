Questions, answered

Straight answers.

No. A reservation reduces available units and holds your place while you pay. Ownership is created only when funds clear, documents are signed and the legal issuance is recorded in the project company's register.
Distributions are paid only from legally distributable profit and available cash, after costs and reserves. Reports are monthly; payouts follow an approved monthly cycle — never automatic, never guaranteed.
Holdings are illiquid — there is no public market. Transfers are possible to an eligible buyer under documented rules, subject to lock-ups and approvals. Plan for the full holding period.
We use all-or-nothing closing. If the long-stop date passes without full funding, the offer is cancelled and collected subscriptions are returned under the disclosed refund policy.
