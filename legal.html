Terms of use

This demonstration website is provided for evaluation. Nothing on it is an offer, solicitation or financial advice. Accounts, reservations, payments and figures are illustrative and create no legal obligation. We may change or withdraw demo content at any time. You must not rely on demo data for real decisions.

Privacy notice

We collect only what we need to run the platform: identity and contact details, eligibility evidence, and transaction records. Data is used for onboarding, compliance screening, reporting and service messages. We never sell personal data. Production systems will store data in-region with defined retention and deletion schedules, and you may request access, correction or deletion at any time.

⚠ Risk statement

Investing in commercial projects involves significant risk:

  • Capital at risk. You may get back less than you invest, including a total loss.
  • Illiquidity. There is no public market for holdings; exits depend on documented transfer, sale or wind-up processes.
  • No guaranteed returns. Distributions depend on real profit and cash; past illustrations are not indicative of future results.
  • Concentration. Single-asset projects carry single-asset risk.
  • Operator & counterparty risk. Performance depends on operators, tenants and service providers.
  • Regulatory change. Rules affecting the structure, tax or enforceability of holdings may change.

Complaints procedure

If something goes wrong, tell us — complaints make the platform better:

  • Send your complaint with your ticket or investor reference via the contact page.
  • We acknowledge within 2 business days.
  • We resolve within 15 business days, or explain the delay and give a new date.
  • Every complaint is logged, reviewed by management monthly, and reported in governance summaries.
