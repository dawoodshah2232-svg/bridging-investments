Governance

Controls you can audit.

⚖ Maker–checker

Every sensitive action — payouts, offer publication, register changes — is prepared by one person and approved by another. The system enforces it; the audit log proves it.

🔒 Segregation of duties

Compliance never reports to sales. Finance never releases what it prepares. Admin access never touches balances.

📋 Conflicts policy

Sponsor holdings, related-party operators and fee recipients are disclosed per project. Where a conflict exists, it is documented and managed — never hidden.

🏛 Regulated path

We operate only inside approved permissions, with money movement through an authorised partner. No live commitments are taken before approvals are in place.
